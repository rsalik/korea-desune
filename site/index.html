When the leaves turn

Each bar runs from first color to the end of peak, with the darker part marking the peak window. The shaded band is your trip, Oct 17 to 25. A marker on a row means this route visits there on those dates. The Korea Forest Service calls it "peak" when about 50% of the leaves have colored; Weather i uses about 80%, so the windows here are drawn to span both.
